<h1 class="custom-page-title">Research</h1>

  <div class="research-hero">
    <div class="research-label">Research Overview</div>
    <p>
      My research lies at the intersection of quantitative finance, derivatives, risk management,
      and computational finance. I am particularly interested in how option-implied information
      can be used to study market expectations, tail risk, density forecasting, and financial risk
      measurement.
    </p>
    <p>
      As my current papers are still in progress, this page provides a high-level overview of my
      research interests rather than detailed descriptions of unpublished models, empirical results,
      or implementation choices.
    </p>
  </div>

  <h2>Research Interests</h2>

  <div class="research-grid">

    <div class="research-card">
      <h3>Option-Implied Information</h3>
      <p>
        I study information embedded in option prices and implied distributions, with a focus on
        how market expectations and risk-neutral measures can be used in empirical finance.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="research-card">
      <h3>Tail Risk and Market Risk</h3>
      <p>
        I am interested in tail behaviour, downside risk, and market risk measurement, especially
        in settings where extreme outcomes and distributional asymmetry are important.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="research-card">
      <h3>Density Forecasting</h3>
      <p>
        My work considers the evaluation of predictive densities and risk measures, including
        calibration, scoring, and risk-management-oriented assessment.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="research-card">
      <h3>Computational Finance</h3>
      <p>
        I also work on numerical and computational methods for derivative pricing, implied
        distribution recovery, and financial modelling.
      </p>
    </div>

  </div>

  <h2>Current Research Directions</h2>

  <div class="research-list">

    <div class="research-item">
      <h3>Option-Implied Tail Risk and Market Conditions</h3>
      <p>
        This line of research studies how option-implied distributional information can be used
        to understand downside risk and market conditions. The current version is under development.
      </p>
      <span class="research-status">Status: Working paper in progress</span>
    </div>

    <div class="research-item">
      <h3>Density Forecasting and Financial Risk Measurement</h3>
      <p>
        This project focuses on option-implied density forecasting and the evaluation of financial
        risk measures from a risk-management perspective.
      </p>
      <span class="research-status">Status: Working paper in progress</span>
    </div>

    <div class="research-item">
      <h3>Sector-Level Option-Implied Information</h3>
      <p>
        This research direction explores how option-implied information may vary across sectors
        and whether sector-level derivatives markets provide additional insights beyond broad
        market index options.
      </p>
      <span class="research-status">Status: Early-stage research</span>
    </div>

    <div class="research-item">
      <h3>Numerical Methods for Implied Distribution Recovery</h3>
      <p>
        I am also interested in numerical approaches for recovering and analysing implied
        distributions from derivative prices, with potential applications in computational finance
        and risk management.
      </p>
      <span class="research-status">Status: Methodological research in progress</span>
    </div>

  </div>

  <h2>Research Approach</h2>

  <div class="research-approach">
    <p>
      My research combines theoretical modelling, empirical analysis, numerical methods, and
      programming-based implementation. I work mainly with derivatives data, option-implied
      distributions, risk-neutral and physical measures, and evaluation methods for density
      forecasts and risk measures.
    </p>
  </div>
